The concept of the "Big Telly" may only be familiar to those of a certain age. Those with a childhood devoid of portable gaming devices, but where physically lugging your cherished console or computer down from your bedroom so you could experience the majesty of playing Sensible Soccer on the huge (for the time) living room "Big Telly" was a thing of joy.
